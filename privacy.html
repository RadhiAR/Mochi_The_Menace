Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This website is a personal fan page for Mochi, a dog, run by her owner ("we"). We keep it simple and collect as little as possible.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the "Ask Mochi's mom" form: your email address, your question, and, if you add them, your name and details about your dog.

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts. We don't ask you to create an account.

How we use it

We use your question and email to read and reply to you. If your question could help other dog parents, we may publish it on our FAQ page together with our answer and the name you gave us (or "A dog parent" if you left it blank). We never publish your email address. We never sell or share your details for marketing.

If you'd like your published question removed, just ask and we'll delete it.

Who processes it

Form messages are delivered to us by a form service (Formspree) or by your own email app, depending on how the form is set up. That service only handles the message so it can reach us. We keep messages only as long as we need to reply, and delete them on request.

Links to other sites

This site links to Mochi's social media pages and to shops. Some shop links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete anything you've sent us. Reply to any email from us, or send a message through the form on the Dog FAQ page, and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
