About Mochi

Hi, I’m Mochi! I’m a terrier cross, and Mom thinks I’m mostly Jack Russell, because just look at me. I’m a rescue pup, so my family tree is a little bit of a mystery, but Mom says that just makes me one of a kind. I started my story in the USA, then packed my squeakiest toy and moved to Canada, where I’ve been sniffing out every park, trail and squirrel ever since. I’m a very fit girl and I love being outside. Give me a park, some playtime with my doggy friends, and I’m the happiest pup around. Squirrels? I watch them. I study them. Then I chase them. I haven’t caught one yet, but I’m working on it. Mom says I’m “gregarious.” I think that means I like everybody, and it’s true! I make friends wherever I go and I can settle in anywhere. I have a big attitude and plenty of teenage sass... right up until something surprises me, and then I’m a little bit shy. Mom says I’m cuteness overloaded. I agree with Mom.

About her mom

My mom is a software developer, which means she types on her computer a lot while I supervise from her feet. She loves her fur baby more than anything (she tells me every day), takes all my photos and shares the best snacks. When she isn’t coding, she’s crocheting cozy little things, and she even has her very own crochet design page. I’m not allowed to chase the yarn. I chase the yarn.
