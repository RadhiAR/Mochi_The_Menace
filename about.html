About Mochi

Mochi got her name because she's soft, sweet and impossible to resist. She earned the second half of her name, the Menace, almost immediately. She has strong opinions about bath time, a sixth sense for snacks being unwrapped anywhere in the house, and a real talent for sitting innocently next to things she has very clearly destroyed.

About her mom

Her mom is the person behind the camera, the treat pouch and every apology to the neighbors. She started posting Mochi's daily chaos for friends and family, and it grew into a small community of dog parents swapping stories, wins and the occasional chewed-shoe confession.

Along the way, feeding Mochi became its own adventure, with picky phases, tummy troubles and a lot of trial and error with the vet. That's why this site exists: to share what worked, the things we actually use, and of course, the evidence.
